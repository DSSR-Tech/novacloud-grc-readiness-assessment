Project assumptions

The assessment assumes that:

• NovaCloud Solutions operates exclusively on Microsoft Azure.

• The company follows a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business model.

• Customer information includes confidential business information and personal data.

• Existing security controls are partially implemented.

• Documentation available to the assessment team is limited.

• The engagement is performed without interrupting production services.

• Technical validation activities are limited to documentation review and simulated evidence.
