# Engagement Timeline

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Project Phases

| Phase | Activity | Primary Deliverables |
|-------|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | Project Initiation | Project Scope, Project Assumptions, Engagement Timeline |
| 2 | Company Assessment | Company Profile, Business Context |
| 3 | Asset Identification | Asset Inventory, Information Classification |
| 4 | Risk Assessment | Risk Register, Risk Matrix |
| 5 | Compliance Assessment | ISO/IEC 27001 Assessment, ENS Assessment |
| 6 | Gap Analysis | Compliance Gap Analysis, Findings |
| 7 | Remediation Planning | Remediation Plan, Recommended Actions |
| 8 | Reporting | Audit Evidence, Power BI Dashboard, Final Assessment Report |

## Engagement Approach

The engagement follows a phased approach in which each activity builds upon the outputs of the previous phase.

Each phase concludes with the review of its corresponding deliverables before the engagement progresses to the next stage.

## Expected Deliverables

Upon completion of the engagement, NovaCloud Solutions will have:

- A documented inventory of information assets.
- A risk register aligned with business objectives.
- An assessment of compliance against ISO/IEC 27001 and ENS.
- A documented remediation plan addressing identified gaps.
- A consolidated set of audit evidence.
- An executive compliance dashboard.
- A final readiness assessment report.
