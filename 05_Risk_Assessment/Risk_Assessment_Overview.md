# Risk Assessment Overview

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

The risk assessment evaluates risks affecting the information, systems and business services within the scope of the engagement.

The assessment builds on the asset inventory and information classification activities completed in previous phases and provides the basis for prioritizing risks and defining appropriate treatment actions.

## Assessment Approach

Risks are identified based on the assets and business processes included in the assessment scope. Each risk is evaluated considering its potential impact on NovaCloud Solutions and the likelihood of the relevant event occurring.

The assessment considers the following factors:

- Business impact
- Information sensitivity
- Operational dependency
- Regulatory and contractual impact
- Existing security controls
- Likelihood of occurrence

The assessment is based on the information available during the engagement, including documented processes, identified assets, security controls and simulated assessment evidence.

## Risk Rating Method

Risk ratings are calculated using two factors: likelihood and impact.

Both factors are rated from 1 to 5. The overall risk score is obtained by multiplying likelihood by impact.

| Score | Risk Level |
|-------|------------|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

The rating is used to support prioritization and does not represent a statement that a risk has been completely eliminated or that a control is fully effective.

## Likelihood Criteria

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Almost Certain |

## Impact Criteria

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Limited impact on business operations or information |
| 2 | Minor impact requiring limited corrective action |
| 3 | Moderate impact affecting business processes or information |
| 4 | Significant impact affecting critical services, sensitive information or regulatory obligations |
| 5 | Severe impact with major operational, regulatory, financial or reputational consequences |

## Risk Treatment

The identified risks are assigned a treatment approach based on their rating and business context.

Possible treatment options are:

- Mitigate
- Accept
- Transfer
- Avoid

Treatment actions are documented in the Risk Treatment document and tracked together with the responsible owner, priority and current status.

## Assessment Relevance

The risk assessment provides the basis for prioritizing security and compliance improvements within the NovaCloud environment.

The results will be used in subsequent compliance gap analysis and remediation activities, particularly where identified risks are linked to weaknesses in existing controls or documentation.
