# Risk Treatment

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

This document defines the treatment actions for the risks identified in the Risk Register.

The actions are based on the current risk ratings, existing controls and identified weaknesses within the NovaCloud environment.

## Treatment Approach

Risk treatment is focused primarily on reducing identified risks through improvements to existing controls, processes and supporting documentation.

The following treatment options are considered within the assessment:

- **Mitigate** — implement or improve controls to reduce the risk.
- **Accept** — retain the risk based on an appropriate business decision.
- **Transfer** — shift part of the risk to a third party through contractual or other arrangements.
- **Avoid** — remove the activity or condition giving rise to the risk.

The proposed treatment for each risk is recorded in the Risk Register and further actions are documented below.

## Implementation Tracking

Treatment actions should be tracked by the assigned Risk Owner and reviewed as part of the ongoing risk management process.

Progress should be updated when actions are implemented, supporting evidence becomes available or the underlying risk changes.

Completed actions should be supported by appropriate evidence before the related risk is reassessed.

## Risk Review

Risks should be reassessed when significant changes affect the relevant asset, threat, vulnerability or existing controls.

Following implementation of a treatment action, the residual risk should be reassessed and documented in the Risk Register.

## Assessment Relevance

Risk treatment provides the link between the risks identified during the assessment and the remediation activities required to address them.

The treatment actions will also support the subsequent compliance gap analysis and remediation activities within the engagement.
