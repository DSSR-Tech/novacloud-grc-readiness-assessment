## Regulatory Context

NovaCloud Solutions provides cloud-based document management services to organizations operating in sectors where information security, regulatory compliance and service availability are key business requirements.

As a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) provider, the organization must demonstrate that its governance framework and security controls support both customer expectations and applicable regulatory obligations.

This assessment has been commissioned to evaluate the current control environment, identify areas requiring improvement and support future certification initiatives.

---

## Regulatory Landscape

The assessment considers the regulatory and contractual obligations commonly associated with organizations providing cloud services to regulated industries.

The primary reference frameworks are:

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- Esquema Nacional de Seguridad (ENS)

The engagement also considers regulatory principles and contractual security requirements typically expected by enterprise customers, including:

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- Customer-specific information security requirements
- Internal security policies and governance standards

---

## Business Drivers

The assessment supports several strategic objectives defined by NovaCloud Solutions, including:

- Strengthening governance processes
- Improving risk management practices
- Standardizing security documentation
- Increasing audit readiness
- Supporting future certification initiatives
- Reinforcing customer confidence

---

## Compliance Responsibilities

NovaCloud Solutions remains responsible for implementing, operating and maintaining its information security controls.

The role of the assessment team is to evaluate the current governance framework, identify compliance gaps and provide recommendations based on the applicable assessment criteria.

Responsibility for implementing any recommended improvements remains with NovaCloud Solutions.

---

## Assessment Relevance

The regulatory context defines the compliance objectives that drive this engagement and establishes the basis for the governance review, risk assessment and compliance activities described throughout this project.
