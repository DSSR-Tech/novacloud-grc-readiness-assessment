## Business Model

NovaCloud Solutions operates under a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business model, delivering cloud-based document management and workflow automation services through a subscription-based platform.

The organization provides a multi-tenant solution that enables customers to securely manage business documentation, automate approval workflows and maintain centralized repositories for critical information.

Revenue is generated primarily through recurring subscription agreements, complemented by professional services including platform implementation, customer onboarding and technical support.

## Customer Profile

NovaCloud Solutions primarily serves medium-sized and large organizations operating in regulated industries where information security, data protection and regulatory compliance are key business requirements.

Typical customer sectors include:

- Financial Services
- Healthcare
- Insurance
- Public Administration
- Legal Services
- Professional Services

Customer contracts typically include service level agreements (SLAs), technical support and regular platform updates.

## Core Business Services

The platform provides the following core services:

- Secure document storage
- Document version control
- Workflow automation
- Electronic approval processes
- Role-based access control
- Audit logging
- Secure document sharing
- Information retention management

## Critical Business Processes

The following business processes are considered critical to the organization's operations:

- Customer identity and access management
- Document storage and retrieval
- Workflow execution
- Customer onboarding
- Subscription management
- Service monitoring
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Customer support

## Business Dependencies

Business operations depend on the continuous availability of several technology services, including:

- Microsoft Azure
- Microsoft Entra ID
- Microsoft 365
- Azure SQL Database
- Azure Blob Storage
- GitHub Enterprise

Any prolonged disruption affecting these services may impact service availability, customer operations and contractual commitments.
