## Organizational Overview

NovaCloud Solutions employs approximately 3,200 people across Spain and Portugal and operates through a centralized organizational structure supporting business, technology and corporate functions.

The assessment focuses on the departments responsible for information security, technology operations and business processes relevant to the scope of this engagement.

---

## Executive Leadership

Executive management provides strategic direction and is accountable for corporate governance, business performance and information security oversight.

Key executive roles include:

- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Chief Operations Officer (COO)

## Governance Responsibilities

Information security responsibilities are distributed across multiple business units.

The Security & Compliance department defines governance processes, maintains security policies and coordinates compliance initiatives.

Operational responsibility for individual controls remains with the corresponding business and technical teams.

---

## Control Owners

Each security control included within the assessment is assigned to a designated Control Owner.

Control Owners are responsible for:

- Implementing assigned controls
- Maintaining supporting documentation
- Providing audit evidence
- Monitoring control effectiveness
- Coordinating remediation activities where required

Control ownership remains with NovaCloud Solutions throughout the engagement.

The assessment team evaluates the design and implementation of controls but does not assume operational responsibility for them.
