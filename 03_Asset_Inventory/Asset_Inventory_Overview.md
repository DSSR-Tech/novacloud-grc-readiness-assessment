# Asset Inventory Overview

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

Asset identification is an essential activity for understanding the systems, information and services supporting business operations.

This document defines the approach used to identify and categorize the assets supporting NovaCloud Solutions' SaaS platform.

The inventory provides the information required for risk assessment, security control evaluation and compliance activities.

## Asset Identification Scope

The inventory includes assets required for the operation, protection and management of the NovaCloud Solutions platform.

The following asset categories are considered:

- Information assets
- Business applications
- Cloud infrastructure components
- Security services
- Supporting technologies
- External dependencies

## Asset Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Information Assets | Customer information, business data and operational records |
| Applications | Software platforms supporting business processes |
| Infrastructure | Cloud resources and technical components |
| Security Services | Services supporting security monitoring and protection |
| Supporting Assets | Tools and services required for platform operation |

## Asset Identification Criteria

Assets are identified based on their relevance to:

- Business operations
- Information security requirements
- Regulatory obligations
- Service availability
- Data protection requirements

## Asset Ownership

Each identified asset is assigned an owner responsible for ensuring appropriate management and protection.

Asset ownership represents accountability for the asset from a business and security perspective and does not necessarily represent technical administration responsibility.

## Assessment Relevance

The asset inventory provides the foundation for information classification, risk assessment and compliance analysis performed throughout this engagement.
