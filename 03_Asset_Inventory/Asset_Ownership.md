**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

This document defines the ownership model applied to the assets identified within the NovaCloud Solutions environment.

Assigning ownership ensures that each asset has a responsible role accountable for its management, protection and appropriate use.

Asset ownership represents business accountability and does not necessarily correspond to technical administration responsibilities.

## Ownership Model

Assets are assigned an owner based on their business relevance and operational impact.

Asset owners are responsible for:

- Ensuring appropriate protection measures are considered
- Reviewing the relevance and usage of the asset
- Supporting information classification activities
- Participating in risk assessment activities where required

Technical administration responsibilities may be delegated to operational teams while accountability remains with the assigned owner.

## Asset Ownership Roles

The following roles participate in asset ownership activities:

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Executive Management | Provides oversight for business-critical assets |
| Data Protection Officer | Responsible for assets involving personal data and privacy requirements |
| Security & Compliance Manager | Coordinates security-related assets and compliance activities |
| IT Operations Manager | Responsible for infrastructure and operational services |
| Head of Engineering | Responsible for application development platforms and software assets |

## Ownership Assignment

Asset ownership is recorded within the asset register and reviewed when significant changes occur.

Changes that may require ownership review include:

- Introduction of new services or technologies
- Changes to business processes
- Changes in regulatory requirements
- Changes affecting asset usage or criticality

## Assessment Relevance

Clear ownership supports effective risk management, security control implementation and compliance activities throughout this engagement.
