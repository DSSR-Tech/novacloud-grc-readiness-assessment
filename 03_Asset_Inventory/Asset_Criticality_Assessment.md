# Asset Criticality Assessment

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

This document defines the criteria used to evaluate the criticality of the identified assets within the NovaCloud Solutions environment.

The assessment provides an initial view of the importance of each asset based on its impact on business operations, information security requirements and service availability.

The results support prioritization activities during the subsequent risk assessment phase.

## Criticality Evaluation Criteria

Asset criticality is evaluated considering the following factors:

| Criteria | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Business Impact | Impact on business operations if the asset becomes unavailable or compromised |
| Information Sensitivity | Importance and sensitivity of the information managed by the asset |
| Operational Dependency | Dependence of business services on the asset |
| Regulatory Impact | Impact related to legal, regulatory or contractual obligations |

## Criticality Levels

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| High | Assets required for essential business operations or handling sensitive information |
| Medium | Assets supporting business activities where disruption would have a limited operational impact |
| Low | Assets with limited impact on business operations or security requirements |

## Assessment Methodology

Criticality values are assigned based on the combination of business impact, information sensitivity, operational dependency and regulatory considerations.

The assigned values are reviewed when significant changes occur, including:

- Introduction of new services
- Changes in business processes
- Changes affecting asset usage
- Changes in regulatory requirements

## Assessment Relevance

The criticality assessment provides input for risk identification, impact evaluation and the prioritization of security measures.
