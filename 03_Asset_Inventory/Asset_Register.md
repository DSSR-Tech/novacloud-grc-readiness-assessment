## Overview

This document records the resources identified within the scope of the NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment.

The register provides an inventory of the information, systems and supporting services required to operate the NovaCloud Solutions SaaS platform.

This inventory supports information classification, risk assessment and compliance activities performed during the engagement.
