## Overview

This document records the assets identified within the scope of the NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment.

The register provides a structured inventory of the information, systems and supporting services required for the operation of the NovaCloud Solutions SaaS platform.

The asset register will be used throughout this engagement to support information classification, risk assessment and compliance activities.
