# Information Classification Overview

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

Information classification is used to determine the level of protection required for information handled by NovaCloud Solutions.

This document defines the approach used to classify information according to its sensitivity, business value and applicable security requirements.

The classification scheme provides a consistent basis for determining how information should be handled, stored and shared throughout its lifecycle.

## Classification Scope

The classification process covers information handled by NovaCloud Solutions as part of its business and SaaS operations.

This includes:

- Customer information
- Personal data
- Business and operational information
- Financial information
- Security and audit records
- Corporate information
- Technical documentation

## Classification Principles

Information is classified according to its sensitivity and the potential impact of unauthorized disclosure, modification or loss.

Classification decisions take into account:

- Business requirements
- Confidentiality requirements
- Legal and regulatory obligations
- Contractual requirements
- Potential impact of unauthorized disclosure

The assigned classification may be reviewed when the use, content or applicable requirements of the information change.

## Classification Ownership

Information owners are responsible for determining the appropriate classification for the information under their responsibility.

Security & Compliance provides guidance on the classification process and supports the review of classification decisions where required.

## Assessment Relevance

Information classification provides input for the selection of appropriate security controls and supports the subsequent risk assessment and compliance activities.
