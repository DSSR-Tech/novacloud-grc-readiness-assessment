# Information Handling Requirements

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

This document defines the handling requirements associated with the information classification levels used by NovaCloud Solutions.

The requirements apply to the storage, access, transmission and sharing of information and are intended to provide a consistent approach to information handling across the organization.

## Handling Requirements

| Classification | Access | Storage | Transmission | Sharing |
|----------------|--------|---------|--------------|---------|
| Public | No specific access restrictions | Approved corporate or public systems | No additional restrictions | May be shared publicly |
| Internal | Employees and authorized personnel | Approved corporate systems | Company-approved channels | Limited to business purposes |
| Confidential | Authorized personnel with a business need | Approved systems with appropriate access controls | Protected communication channels | Restricted to authorized recipients |
| Restricted | Strictly limited to authorized personnel | Systems with enhanced access and security controls | Encrypted communication channels | Requires explicit authorization |

## Access Requirements

Access to information must be based on business need and appropriate authorization.

Confidential and Restricted information must only be accessible to personnel whose responsibilities require access.

Access permissions should be reviewed periodically and removed when no longer required.

## Storage Requirements

Information must be stored using systems approved by NovaCloud Solutions.

Confidential and Restricted information must not be stored in personal accounts or unauthorized services.

Where applicable, technical controls such as access restrictions, encryption and backup mechanisms must be used to protect stored information.

## Transmission Requirements

Information must be transmitted using approved communication channels.

Confidential and Restricted information must be protected against unauthorized disclosure during transmission.

External transmission of Restricted information requires authorization from the relevant Information Owner.

## Sharing Requirements

Information may only be shared with recipients who have a legitimate business need.

Confidential and Restricted information shared with external parties must be subject to appropriate contractual or security requirements.

Public information may be shared without additional authorization.

## Disposal Requirements

Information must be securely deleted or disposed of when it is no longer required, taking into account applicable retention requirements.

Additional disposal measures may be required for Confidential and Restricted information.

## Review

Handling requirements are reviewed when there are significant changes to business processes, technology, information types or regulatory requirements.

## Assessment Relevance

These requirements provide the basis for evaluating how classified information is handled and protected during the compliance review.
