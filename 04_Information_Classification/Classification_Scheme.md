# Classification Scheme

## Project Information

**Project**  
NovaCloud GRC Readiness Assessment

**Client**  
NovaCloud Solutions

**Assessment Type**  
Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) Readiness Assessment

## Overview

This document defines the information classification levels used by NovaCloud Solutions.

The scheme provides a common basis for determining the protection and handling requirements for different types of information.

## Classification Levels

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Public | Information that can be disclosed without causing harm to NovaCloud Solutions or its customers. |
| Internal | Information intended for use by NovaCloud Solutions employees and authorized personnel. |
| Confidential | Information that could cause business, legal or reputational impact if disclosed without authorization. |
| Restricted | Highly sensitive information requiring strict access controls due to its potential business, legal or security impact. |

## Classification Criteria

Information classification is based on the sensitivity of the information and the potential impact of unauthorized disclosure.

The following factors are considered when assigning a classification:

- Sensitivity of the information
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Contractual obligations
- Business impact
- Customer requirements
- Security considerations

## Classification Examples

The following examples are provided as a general reference:

| Classification | Examples |
|----------------|----------|
| Public | Public website content, press releases, published product information |
| Internal | Internal procedures, operational documentation, internal communications |
| Confidential | Customer information, business plans, financial information, contracts |
| Restricted | Authentication credentials, encryption keys, privileged access information, sensitive security records |

The examples above are indicative. The appropriate classification is determined based on the content and context of the information.

## Handling Requirements

Handling requirements increase with the sensitivity of the information.

Restricted and Confidential information requires controlled access and must only be shared with authorized personnel or third parties with a legitimate business need.

Public information may be shared without additional access restrictions.

Detailed handling requirements are defined separately within the Information Handling Requirements document.

## Classification Review

Information classifications are reviewed when significant changes occur, including:

- Changes to the content or intended use of the information
- Changes in legal or regulatory requirements
- Changes in contractual requirements
- Changes in business processes

## Assessment Relevance

The classification scheme provides a consistent basis for the classification register and supports the subsequent risk assessment and compliance activities.
